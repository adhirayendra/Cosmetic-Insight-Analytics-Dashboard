# Cosmetic Insight Analytics Dashboard

🇮🇩 Dashboard interaktif berbasis Looker Studio (Google Data Studio) untuk menganalisis performa penjualan produk kosmetik lintas negara, produk, dan sales person.

🇬🇧 An interactive dashboard built with Looker Studio (Google Data Studio) to analyze cosmetic product sales performance across countries, products, and sales persons.

![Cosmetic Insight Analytics Dashboard](cosmetic_insight_dashboard.png)

## 📊 Overview

**🇮🇩** Dashboard ini terdiri dari 3 halaman analisis yang saling melengkapi — overview umum, insight per sales person, dan insight per negara — untuk membantu tim bisnis memantau revenue, distribusi penjualan, dan performa individu/tim secara komprehensif.

**🇬🇧** This dashboard consists of 3 complementary analysis pages — general overview, sales person insight, and country insight — helping business teams monitor revenue, sales distribution, and individual/team performance comprehensively.

## 📄 Halaman / Pages

### 1️⃣ Cosmetic Insight Analytics Dashboard (Overview)
- 🇮🇩 Ringkasan revenue keseluruhan, ranking revenue per produk, tren revenue bulanan, pembagian revenue per negara, dan revenue per sales person
- 🇬🇧 Overall revenue summary, product revenue ranking, monthly revenue trend, revenue share by country, and revenue by sales person

**KPI:** Total Revenue $959m • Total Boxes 91k • Revenue Per Boxes $10k • Revenue Per Orders $3m • Revenue Per Day $5m

### 2️⃣ Sales Person Insight Analytics Dashboard
- 🇮🇩 Fokus performa individu sales — heatmap revenue per produk, tren revenue bulanan per sales person, dan perbandingan revenue vs boxes shipped
- 🇬🇧 Focused on individual sales performance — revenue-per-product heatmap, monthly revenue trend per sales person, and revenue vs. boxes shipped comparison

**KPI:** Total Revenue $959m • Total Boxes 91k • Average Revenue $3m • Max Revenue 12m • Min Revenue 1k

### 3️⃣ Country Insight Analytics Dashboard
- 🇮🇩 Fokus performa per negara — heatmap revenue per produk & negara, tren revenue bulanan per negara, dan perbandingan revenue vs boxes shipped per negara
- 🇬🇧 Focused on country-level performance — revenue-per-product & country heatmap, monthly revenue trend per country, and revenue vs. boxes shipped comparison by country

**KPI:** Total Revenue $959m • Total Boxes 91k • Average Revenue $3m • Max Revenue 12m • Min Revenue 1k
